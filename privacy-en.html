1.Introduction

Tamyyaz is an Android study and focus app for school and university students around the world, from grade 7 through university. It helps you organize your study time, track your progress, and use AI-powered tools such as lesson summarization, question generation, and translation.

This policy applies to the app whether you installed it from Google Play or downloaded it as an APK directly from our official website. By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of your data as described below.

2.Data we collect

To run the app and its features, we collect the following types of data:

  • Account information: your name, an email address or login identifier (in most cases a school-issued identifier rather than a personal real email), and a password — passwords are fully encrypted via Firebase Authentication, and we cannot see or store them in plain text.
  • Profile information: grade level, school name, class/section, and an optional profile picture (entirely optional — you can use the app without one).
  • Usage activity and academic progress: the duration and timing of study sessions, a history of questions, answers, and mistakes (to help you track weak areas), your in-app "Crystal" balance (an internal motivational currency with no real monetary value), and badges/achievements you earn.

3.Social features and discussions

The app includes social features that let you connect with other students: a friends list, a public forum, and per-lesson discussion threads. Please note that:

  • Public forum posts and lesson discussion messages are not private conversations — they can be read by other students, either from your own school/class or more broadly depending on the section you post in.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal information (phone number, address, family details) in these areas.
  • Your friends list may let you see certain study-related activity or stats of your friends (such as study hours), depending on the privacy settings available in the app.

4.Parent accounts and supervision

Tamyyaz lets a parent or guardian link their own account to a student's account. Once linked, the parent can:

  • View the student's study statistics (study time, progress, homework).
  • Control the "Focus Lock" feature, which restricts the student's use of other apps during study time.

This linking is designed to help parents transparently follow their child's academic progress, and it does not give the parent access to the student's private messages or password.

5.AI features (Gemini)

When you use the app's AI features — such as asking the "smart tutor," requesting a translation, or generating practice questions — the text you write is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing so it can generate an appropriate response.

This means Google acts as a third-party data processor for that specific text (only the text you submit to that feature, not all of your data). This processing is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We recommend avoiding sensitive personal information (such as a phone number or address) in these requests.

6.Where your data is stored

Your data (account, files, activity history) is stored on cloud infrastructure provided by:

  • Google Firebase — for Authentication, the Firestore database, and file Storage.
  • Supabase (Edge Functions) — for certain additional cloud processing and functions.

Both providers use recognized cloud data centers and apply standard industry security practices.

7.Privacy for minors and students

Tamyyaz is primarily aimed at school students, and a large share of our users are minors (under 18). We take this seriously:

  • We do not collect sensitive data beyond what is necessary (such as precise location or direct payment information from a minor student).
  • Student accounts are, in most cases, created through the school or with a parent/guardian's knowledge or supervision, rather than fully independently.
  • We specifically built the parent-linking feature (see section 4 above) so a parent or guardian can follow and supervise their child's use of the app.
  • If you are a parent or teacher and believe a minor's data was collected inappropriately, contact us immediately (section 12 below) and we will review the matter and delete the data if warranted.

8.Data sharing — do we sell it?

No, never. We do not sell user data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising outside the app.

Data is only shared with the technical service providers necessary to run the app itself: Google Firebase, Supabase, and the Google Gemini API (for the AI features described in section 5), and only to the extent needed to operate each feature.

9.Data security

We apply reasonable security practices to protect your data: passwords are encrypted via Firebase Authentication, and the connection between the app and our servers is protected via HTTPS. As with any digital service, no system is 100% secure, and we encourage you to keep your password confidential.

10.Your rights over your data

You may ask us at any time to:

  • Access the data stored about your account.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and all associated data from our systems.

To make any of these requests, contact us via section 12 below.

11.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's features or new legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the latest published version. If a change is significant, we will try to notify you through the app itself.
